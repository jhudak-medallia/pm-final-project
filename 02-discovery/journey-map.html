Journey Map: The High-Volume Delivery Driver

RouteLogic Velocity · Module 2 Discovery · One shift, five stages, and the workarounds drivers use at each one

Role

A frontline delivery driver who runs 40+ stops a day and spends their whole shift in the RouteLogic mobile app.

Goal

Get through every stop quickly and with confidence, without fighting the app at each doorstep.

Friction

Feature bloat has buried core actions: "Start Route" (~30 uses a day) sits 2–3 levels deep, and "Mark Delivered" takes 3 taps across 3 screens.

Why it matters to the business

Usage decline

Drivers still deliver, just not through RouteLogic.

Deliveries, exceptions, and route changes now live in WhatsApp, on paper, and in phone calls.

Renewal risk

The reporting customers buy RouteLogic for is fed by driver data that's late, incomplete, or missing.

Executives see bad dashboards and question the investment.

Cost moves to dispatch

Every driver text becomes manual entry for a dispatcher.

This matches the largest enterprise account calling RouteLogic "an administrative burden."

Competitive opening

If drivers already get by with a phone and a paper list, a leaner routing-focused tool is an easy switch.

The largest enterprise account is already trialing one.
